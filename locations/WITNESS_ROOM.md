# Witness Room

## Physical concept
A narrow interstitial chamber between the library and west stair, reachable only by understanding Celeste’s acoustic routing plan.

## Original function
Fire-resistant document / maintenance cavity combined with acoustic access.

## Narrative function
Contains:
- Celeste’s unsent solicitor letter.
- Sebastian’s 1956 unsent letter.

It must not feel like a magical secret room. Its dimensions, ventilation and access should make architectural sense.
