# Revision Checklist

**Serious manuscript revision: complete.**

This checklist records the structural/developmental revision standard reached in the `serious-revision` pass. A later external beta read and copy edit may still suggest line-level refinements; that is a publication stage, not unfinished story architecture.

## Mystery
- [x] Every primary clue is visible before its decisive payoff.
- [x] Early clues retain at least one plausible non-final interpretation when introduced.
- [x] No major historical conclusion depends on one unsupported document; the final mechanism is corroborated across architecture, civil records, photographs, work records, medical notes, audio, letters and title papers.
- [x] Red herrings resolve fairly, including Harcourt, Sebastian’s diary line, Adrian’s duplicate key and the apparently supernatural voices.
- [x] Reader knowledge is kept within Claire’s evidence path and does not jump ahead accidentally.
- [x] Felix’s present-day sabotage is explicitly separated from Celeste’s 1931 death and the historic title fraud.
- [x] The final unexplained auditory event occurs only after the earlier hauntings have received rational explanations.

## Character
- [x] Claire’s risks arise from professional logic and documented precautions rather than genre-driven recklessness.
- [x] Adrian’s secrecy is specific, understandable and ethically consequential: he controls uncertainty that should have belonged to Claire’s decision-making.
- [x] Talia’s silence has a coherent protective logic without being excused.
- [x] Evelyn’s fear, youth and precarious position are legible without excusing the gate removal, confrontation or later silence.
- [x] Sebastian’s cowardice is demonstrated through choices: secrecy, bigamy, incomplete testimony and authorisation of the fraudulent transfer.
- [x] Sophie, Imogen, Jonah, Elspeth, Harcourt and Felix retain distinct functions rather than becoming interchangeable exposition devices.

## Romance
- [x] Attraction grows through competence, humour, ordinary work and selective vulnerability, not only danger.
- [x] The first kiss follows an earlier almost-kiss in which Claire chooses to step back and Adrian respects the boundary.
- [x] Physical intimacy is explicitly Claire’s choice and is not treated as forgiveness for Adrian’s omission.
- [x] The core betrayal is informed consent / professional integrity, not a cheap misunderstanding.
- [x] Separation is maintained through the climax rather than being erased by danger.
- [x] Reconciliation follows Adrian’s costly public/legal actions and sustained changed behaviour, not another apology.
- [x] Claire remains legally, professionally and residentially independent in the ending.

## Plausibility
- [x] The 1923 land purchase is treated as an unregistered-land conveyance rather than a modern Land Registry entry.
- [x] The 1932 deed problem is separated from the much more complex question of modern registered title.
- [x] Miriam’s practical care of Eleanor is not casually equated with automatic legal guardianship.
- [x] Historic civil-register mechanics were corrected: index, certified copy and archival-transfer issues are kept distinct.
- [x] Hazardous-material and service-void access is handled with stop-work / competent-survey logic where required.
- [x] Acoustic phenomena are produced by plausible overlapping speaking tubes, cavities, ventilation and later building alterations rather than one impossible whole-house system.
- [x] Police handling of Felix’s admissions includes a caution and offer of legal advice once he becomes a suspect.
- [x] Felix’s financial motive relies on public filings and documents he supplied to the estate, not implausible access to private bank records.

## Prose and experience
- [x] British English is the house style.
- [x] Raven House feels architecturally specific rather than generically spooky.
- [x] Major repeated gothic clichés and production-code leakage were removed from reader-facing prose.
- [x] Exposition is distributed through conflict, work and discovery rather than loaded into explanatory dialogue alone.
- [x] The mystery becomes more interesting as the supernatural appearance is rationalised.
- [x] The final echo remains honestly unclassified rather than being over-explained.

## Production
- [x] Thirty-five chapter sources remain the canonical manuscript.
- [x] `tools/build_manuscript.py` compiles only chapter titles and `## Draft` prose into a clean reader manuscript.
- [x] `tools/continuity_check.py` checks sequence, duplicate titles, production leaks, banned clichés and Draft-only word counts.
- [x] Export instructions no longer send production chapter metadata directly to Pandoc.
