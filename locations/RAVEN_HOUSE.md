# Raven House

## Narrative function
Raven House is not merely a backdrop; it is a physical argument about concealment, control, authorship and memory.

## Architectural principles
- Built 1923–1925 under Celeste Vane’s design authority.
- Exterior and interior dimensions do not always reconcile because service/acoustic voids occupy thickness between rooms.
- Experimental acoustic channels include speaking tubes, ventilation paths, resonant cavities and maintenance routes.
- Hidden spaces must always have plausible original functions.
- Alterations by later Ravens obscure Celeste’s original plan.

## Key zones
- West Wing — impossible dimensions; hidden void.
- West Stair — fatal landing; former removable brass safety gate.
- Drafting Room — Celeste’s working space and sealed niche.
- Nursery — marked “E.” on original plan.
- Library — access point for acoustic mapping.
- Witness Room — inaccessible interstitial chamber containing final documents.
- Service passages — practical access routes later mythologized as ghost corridors.

## Atmosphere palette
Stone, lime plaster, oak, oxidised brass, rain-dark glass, drafts, resonant voids, paper dust, old wax, iron fixings, muffled footfall.

## Avoid
Generic haunted-house décor, endless cobwebs, unexplained locked doors without architectural logic, or making every room “cold.”
