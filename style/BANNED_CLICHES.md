# Banned / Flagged Clichés

Flag for revision unless deliberately subverted:
- a chill ran down her spine
- blood ran cold
- heart skipped a beat
- breath she didn’t know she was holding
- deafening silence
- old house groaned
- eyes darkened
- electricity between them
- time stood still
- like a moth to a flame
- secrets buried in the walls
- if these walls could talk
- pregnant pause
- jaw clenched
- released a breath
- she shivered, though not from the cold

Also flag repeated use of:
`suddenly`, `somehow`, `very`, `just`, `really`, `felt`, `seemed`, `began to`.
