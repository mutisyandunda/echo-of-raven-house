# Claire / Adrian Romance Architecture

1. **Chapters 1–4 — friction:** attraction noticed but unwelcome; conflict over information and family history.
2. **Chapters 5–9 — respect:** they are drawn together through competence, courage and shared problem-solving.
3. **Chapters 10–13 — intimacy:** late-night mapping, shared food, dry humour, personal disclosures; almost-kiss in Chapter 13.
4. **Chapter 15 — first kiss:** follows an honest conversation, not a danger spike.
5. **Chapters 18–21 — opening:** they become allies; after the marriage reveal they allow the relationship to deepen. First full physical intimacy in Chapter 20 away from Raven House.
6. **Chapters 23–25 — fracture:** Claire learns Adrian partly hired her because Marcus named “Mercer.” She experiences this as professional manipulation and compromised consent.
7. **Chapters 26–32 — separation under pressure:** they continue protecting each other but do not resume intimacy. Adrian proves change through conduct, not pleading.
8. **Chapters 33–34 — earned reconciliation:** Adrian chooses full disclosure despite risk to the estate; Claire accepts the choice, not merely the apology.
9. **Chapter 35 — together, independent:** relationship survives without absorbing Claire’s professional identity.
