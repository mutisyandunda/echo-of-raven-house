# West Stair

## Function
The fatal site and the book’s most important physical evidence location.

## Canonical features
- Open landing edge originally protected by a removable brass safety gate.
- Gate appears in a pre-death newspaper photograph.
- Carpenter’s daybook confirms removal on Evelyn’s instruction.
- Later alteration makes the missing gate look intentional rather than suspicious.
- Screw/fixing holes remain detectable.
- Dark staining is mineral/iron, not blood.

## Dramatic uses
- Chapter 8: Claire nearly falls at the oddly open landing.
- Chapter 28: photo + carpenter record convert architecture into culpability.
- Chapter 32: Sebastian’s letter explains the fatal movement sequence.
