# Revision Checklist

## Mystery
- [ ] Every clue is visible before its payoff.
- [ ] Every clue is interpretable in at least two ways when introduced.
- [ ] No reveal depends on a single unsupported document.
- [ ] Red herrings resolve fairly.
- [ ] Reader knowledge never jumps ahead accidentally.
- [ ] Felix’s present-day sabotage remains separable from historical guilt.

## Character
- [ ] Claire’s risks arise from professional logic, not genre stupidity.
- [ ] Adrian’s secrecy is specific and ethically consequential.
- [ ] Talia’s silence always has a comprehensible protective logic.
- [ ] Evelyn’s fear is legible without excusing her.
- [ ] Sebastian’s cowardice is shown in choices, not labels.

## Romance
- [ ] Attraction grows through competence and intimacy, not only danger.
- [ ] First kiss follows honesty.
- [ ] Betrayal is about informed consent / professional integrity.
- [ ] Reconciliation is earned through Adrian’s public action.
- [ ] Claire remains professionally independent.

## Prose
- [ ] British English consistent.
- [ ] Raven House feels specific, not generically spooky.
- [ ] Avoid repeated chill/shiver/cold/dark clichés.
- [ ] Dialogue does not carry unnecessary exposition.
- [ ] Architecture remains physically plausible.
