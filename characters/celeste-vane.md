# Celeste Vane

- **Age:** 33 at death
- **Role:** Architect / legal owner of Raven House

## Presentation
Brilliant, disciplined, modern, professionally ambitious. Wanted recognition without surrendering autonomy.

## Secret / hidden information
Secret legal wife of Sebastian; mother of Eleanor; legal owner of Raven House; planned to expose the truth in 1931.

## Arc
Posthumously shifts from forgotten architect / presumed mistress to moral and intellectual centre of the novel.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
