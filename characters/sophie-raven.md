# Sophie Raven

- **Age:** 36
- **Role:** Adrian’s younger sister / documentary photographer

## Presentation
Restless, witty, skeptical of family mythology. Left Raven House because silence made her feel unreal.

## Secret / hidden information
Knows Marcus corresponded with someone named Mercer.

## Arc
Becomes Claire’s ally and forces Adrian to confront what selective honesty does to trust.
