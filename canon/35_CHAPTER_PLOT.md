# 35-Chapter Plot

## 1. The Arrival
Claire arrives in rain. Adrian gives a strict brief: catalogue Celeste’s archive before sale. Raven House seems larger outside than in.

- **Clue movement:** C1 seed
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Friction; attraction/irritation
- **Exit hook:** Bricked doorway absent from modern plan.

## 2. The Measure of the House
Claire proves west-wing dimensions do not reconcile. Celeste’s plans contain tiny repeated echo-like marks.

- **Clue movement:** C1, C2
- **Red herring:** R1
- **Romance beat:** Competent sparring
- **Exit hook:** A voice travels through a wall.

## 3. Talia Raven
Talia questions Claire, freezes at the surname Mercer and claims Celeste left years before her death.

- **Clue movement:** C4 seed
- **Red herring:** R9
- **Romance beat:** Adrian privately apologises without undermining Talia
- **Exit hook:** Talia asks whether Claire’s grandmother was from the north.

## 4. Behind the Panel
Claire and Jonah remove paneling and find Celeste’s initials dated 1931. Disturbed dust suggests recent passage use.

- **Clue movement:** C4, C22 seed
- **Red herring:** R2
- **Romance beat:** Adrian shifts toward ally
- **Exit hook:** Someone has been inside the wall void recently.

## 5. The Echo Line
Claire maps acoustic effects; Elspeth warns that the house repeats what it hears.

- **Clue movement:** C2
- **Red herring:** R1, R2
- **Romance beat:** Shared humour while testing speaking points
- **Exit hook:** One echo carries the name 'Eleanor'.

## 6. Family Dinner
Sophie and Felix arrive. Harcourt says title is outside Claire’s remit. Felix pushes the financial urgency of sale.

- **Clue movement:** C21 seed
- **Red herring:** R3
- **Romance beat:** Attraction becomes socially visible
- **Exit hook:** Felix jokes the house may be worth more demolished.

## 7. The Nursery
Original plan shows nursery marked 'E'. A silver brush engraved E.V. is found.

- **Clue movement:** C3
- **Red herring:** R9
- **Romance beat:** Adrian shares a vulnerable childhood memory
- **Exit hook:** Talia’s lie about there never being a child becomes obvious.

## 8. The West Stair
Claire follows footsteps at night and nearly falls at the oddly open landing. Filled screw holes show a missing fitting.

- **Clue movement:** C14/C15 seed, C22
- **Red herring:** R1
- **Romance beat:** Physical closeness without kiss
- **Exit hook:** The stair was designed to have something where empty space now is.

## 9. The Drafting Room
A narrow void leads to Celeste’s sealed work niche and a photo of Celeste, Miriam and an infant.

- **Clue movement:** C6
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Strong physical tension
- **Exit hook:** Photo inscription references 'our little E.'

## 10. Outside the House
Imogen locates the 1923 land record naming Celeste as owner. Adrian drives Claire to the county archive.

- **Clue movement:** C5
- **Red herring:** R3
- **Romance beat:** Respect deepens away from house
- **Exit hook:** Harcourt calls immediately after the title request.

## 11. The Owner
Adrian absorbs that Raven House may never have been legally Raven property. Claire explains provenance and title logic.

- **Clue movement:** C5 develops
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Late takeaway over blueprints
- **Exit hook:** A plan route ends at a room missing from later drawings.

## 12. The False Pedigree
Claire catches Talia burning a family tree. Adrian backs Claire despite pressure from Harcourt.

- **Clue movement:** C20 seed
- **Red herring:** R4, R3
- **Romance beat:** Adrian chooses Claire’s investigation over family comfort
- **Exit hook:** Burned edge reads 'Vane — issue unknown.'

## 13. If He Will Not Name Her
Celeste’s letter to Miriam reveals she will name the child if Sebastian will not.

- **Clue movement:** C7
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Almost-kiss; Claire pulls back
- **Exit hook:** Letter references a registry copy.

## 14. The Missing Register
Parish trail fails, but Imogen finds the civil registry index for Vane–Raven, 1924.

- **Clue movement:** C8
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Drink together; emotional closeness
- **Exit hook:** The marriage certificate itself is missing.

## 15. First Kiss
A hollow drawing tube contains a key. Claire and Adrian talk honestly about reliance and trust.

- **Clue movement:** C9 setup
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** First deliberate kiss
- **Exit hook:** The key opens a decorative desk drawer.

## 16. The Name Mercer
The drawer contains correspondence; Talia admits Mercer is familiar but refuses context.

- **Clue movement:** C20 seed
- **Red herring:** R9
- **Romance beat:** Claire asks whether her surname mattered; Adrian says no in a technically true but incomplete way
- **Exit hook:** Talia tells Adrian his father should have burned the note.

## 17. The Tube Within the Desk
A second rolled-plan compartment contains a certified 1924 marriage copy.

- **Clue movement:** C9
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Claire anchors a shaken Adrian
- **Exit hook:** Celeste was Sebastian’s legal wife four years before Evelyn.

## 18. The Marriage
Imogen authenticates the marriage; a linked birth index identifies Eleanor Vane, born 1926.

- **Clue movement:** C10
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** They openly choose investigation and relationship
- **Exit hook:** Eleanor later carries surname Mercer.

## 19. Find Mercer
Adrian shows Marcus’s sealed note: 'If you ever sell Raven House, find Mercer first.'

- **Clue movement:** C11
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** First trust rupture
- **Exit hook:** The note includes initials E.M.

## 20. The Registry Road
Claire and Adrian verify records away from the estate; warmth returns briefly.

- **Clue movement:** C10/C11 develop
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** First full physical intimacy
- **Exit hook:** Eleanor’s next of kin is Miriam, not Sebastian.

## 21. The Missing Box
An archive box disappears. Adrian’s duplicate key makes him briefly suspect; modern passage prints point to a human intruder.

- **Clue movement:** C22, C23 seed
- **Red herring:** R8
- **Romance beat:** Claire refuses accusation without evidence
- **Exit hook:** Sophie saw Felix near the estate office.

## 22. The Dead Woman’s Signature
Harcourt produces the 1932 transfer deed; Claire spots Celeste’s signature dated after her death.

- **Clue movement:** C12
- **Red herring:** R3 resolves
- **Romance beat:** Adrian refuses the easy option of stopping the inquiry
- **Exit hook:** The sale cannot proceed cleanly.

## 23. Eleanor Mercer
Miriam’s packet proves Eleanor was Claire’s grandmother.

- **Clue movement:** C6, C7, C10 converge
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Adrian comforts Claire briefly
- **Exit hook:** Claire is Celeste’s direct descendant.

## 24. What Adrian Knew
Sophie reveals Marcus corresponded with Claire’s mother. Adrian admits he recognized the Mercer name when Claire applied.

- **Clue movement:** C20, C11
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Major betrayal; Claire ends relationship
- **Exit hook:** She stays only to protect the archive.

## 25. The Passage
Claire enters the west service route to document it and is deliberately bolted inside.

- **Clue movement:** C22
- **Red herring:** R8
- **Romance beat:** Adrian is frantic but does not use the crisis to force reconciliation
- **Exit hook:** Fresh scraping proves deliberate entrapment.

## 26. The Wrong Raven
Claire and Sophie reconstruct movements; Felix’s story fails and his debts emerge.

- **Clue movement:** C21
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Adrian supports from a respectful distance
- **Exit hook:** Felix borrowed against expected sale proceeds.

## 27. Felix
Felix admits staging footsteps, moving objects, copying a key, stealing the box and trapping Claire.

- **Clue movement:** C21, C22, C23
- **Red herring:** R1/R8 resolve
- **Romance beat:** Adrian chooses accountability
- **Exit hook:** The stolen box contains the carpenter’s daybook.

## 28. Mrs E.A.
The daybook records gate removal on Evelyn’s order; a pre-death photo shows the gate in place.

- **Clue movement:** C14, C15
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Claire and Adrian resume cautious professional cooperation
- **Exit hook:** Evelyn created the fatal physical condition.

## 29. The Wrist
Coroner notes show wrist bruising. Sebastian’s diary line appears damning.

- **Clue movement:** C13
- **Red herring:** R7
- **Romance beat:** Adrian admits fear that his family identity is built on cowardice
- **Exit hook:** Claire concludes Celeste was restrained before the fall.

## 30. Talia’s Tape
Talia produces Evelyn’s 1978 cassette and admits discouraging contact with Claire’s mother.

- **Clue movement:** C16, C20
- **Red herring:** R9 resolves
- **Romance beat:** Talia tells Adrian he is responsible for what he does with inherited lies
- **Exit hook:** Evelyn says she had the gate removed and only meant to stop Celeste.

## 31. The Witness Room
Celeste’s acoustic diagram points to a sealed chamber between library and west stair.

- **Clue movement:** C17
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Claire and Adrian work together without resuming intimacy
- **Exit hook:** A fire-resistant document cavity is found.

## 32. Sebastian’s Letter
The cavity contains Celeste’s unsent solicitor letter and Sebastian’s 1956 unsent confession.

- **Clue movement:** C18, C19
- **Red herring:** R7 resolves
- **Romance beat:** Adrian gives the documents to Claire first
- **Exit hook:** The full death mechanics and cover-up become clear.

## 33. The Choice
Harcourt explains the options. Adrian can bury, settle quietly, or disclose. He chooses full disclosure.

- **Clue movement:** C12/C18/C19 implications
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** His apology becomes action rather than persuasion
- **Exit hook:** Talia signs a statement authenticating the cassette.

## 34. Raven House, Rewritten
The sale is withdrawn; legal settlement and preservation options begin.

- **Clue movement:** All primary clues
- **Red herring:** None
- **Romance beat:** Earned reconciliation and kiss
- **Exit hook:** They agree the house should preserve Celeste’s authorship, not Raven mythology.

## 35. The Echo
Months later the archive supports a heritage trust / research centre while settlement continues.

- **Clue movement:** R10
- **Red herring:** R10
- **Romance beat:** Together but Claire remains independent
- **Exit hook:** Claire hears a woman’s voice in a fully mapped, sealed room with no physical explanation.
