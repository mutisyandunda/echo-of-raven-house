# Canon Rules

## Non-negotiable facts
- Celeste Vane is the architect and legal owner of Raven House.
- Celeste and Sebastian were legally married in 1924.
- Eleanor is their daughter.
- Sebastian’s 1928 marriage to Evelyn was bigamous.
- Evelyn did **not** deliberately push Celeste.
- Evelyn ordered the west stair safety gate removed.
- Evelyn grabbed Celeste during the fatal confrontation.
- Sebastian covered up the death and participated in the forged aftermath.
- Felix is responsible only for present-day sabotage.
- Claire is Celeste’s direct descendant.
- Adrian’s romantic breach is concealment of his hiring motive, not infidelity or generic miscommunication.
- The ending preserves Claire’s professional and legal independence.
- Most supernatural-seeming phenomena are architectural/acoustic.
- One final echo remains unexplained.

## Mystery rules
- No single magic document solves the book.
- Every major reveal needs at least two independent evidence channels.
- Red herrings must be fair and retrospectively explainable.
- Reader knowledge must not exceed POV knowledge without a documentary artifact.
- Clue payoffs should usually occur 6–20 chapters after seeding.

## Prose rules
- Gothic atmosphere comes from architecture, weather, silence, material detail and social tension—not generic horror adjectives.
- Avoid repetitive “cold,” “dark,” “old,” “haunted,” “chill,” and “shiver” language.
- British English throughout.
