# Felix Raven

- **Age:** 38
- **Role:** Adrian’s first cousin

## Presentation
Charming, funny, superficially unserious. Financially desperate and dependent on the proposed sale.

## Secret / hidden information
Stages footsteps, moves objects, copies a key, steals an archive box, and locks Claire in a passage to frighten her away.

## Arc
Functions as the present-day antagonist without contaminating the historical mystery.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
