# Adrian Raven

- **Age:** 39
- **Role:** Owner of Raven House

## Presentation
Calm, dry, competent, emotionally contained. He experiences inheritance as duty rather than glamour.

## Secret / hidden information
His late father left instructions to “find Mercer.” Adrian partly hires Claire because of her surname but withholds this motive.

## Arc
Moves from controlling information to accepting that integrity may require losing the thing he inherited.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
