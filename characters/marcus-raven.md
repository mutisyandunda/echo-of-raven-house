# Marcus Raven

- **Age:** 64 at death (2019)
- **Role:** Adrian’s father

## Presentation
Formal, dutiful, increasingly uneasy about inherited certainty.

## Secret / hidden information
Comes to suspect his inheritance rests on a lie; leaves instruction to find a Mercer before sale.

## Arc
Sets the present-day investigation in motion from beyond the grave.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
