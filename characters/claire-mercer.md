# Claire Mercer

- **Age:** 34
- **Role:** Architectural historian / conservation archivist

## Presentation
Controlled, incisive, observant, professionally fearless. She trusts evidence more easily than people after a senior academic once took credit for her work and damaged her reputation.

## Secret / hidden information
Does not know that her grandmother Eleanor was Celeste Vane’s daughter.

## Arc
Learns to pursue truth without using truth as emotional armour; permits intimacy without surrendering judgment.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
