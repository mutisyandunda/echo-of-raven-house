# Master Story Bible

## Core Story Promise
**Genre:** Gothic romantic mystery / family-secret suspense  
**Setting:** Raven House, near fictional Blackmere, Northumberland, England  
**Present day:** 2026  
**Historical span:** 1922–1984, with later correspondence through 2019  
**Target length:** 85,000–95,000 words  
**POV:** Close third, mainly Claire Mercer  
**Tone:** Elegant, atmospheric, intelligent, sensual rather than melodramatic  
**Core idea:** Architecture functions as forensic evidence. Raven House acts as the third lead character.

## Canonical truth
1. Architect **Celeste Vane** legally and secretly married **Sebastian Raven** in 1924.
2. Celeste bought the Blackmere land and Raven House was legally hers, although the Raven family later claimed it as their estate.
3. Celeste and Sebastian had a daughter, **Eleanor**, in 1926. Eleanor was raised largely by Celeste’s sister Miriam because Sebastian’s father threatened disinheritance if the marriage and child became public.
4. Sebastian publicly married **Evelyn Ashcombe** in 1928 without legally ending his marriage to Celeste.
5. By 1931 Celeste planned to expose the bigamy, reclaim Raven House, acknowledge Eleanor, and challenge Sebastian’s unauthorized use of her property.
6. Evelyn learned of this. On the day Celeste died, Evelyn ordered a removable safety gate on the west stair landing taken away, ostensibly because it spoiled the staircase line.
7. That evening, during a confrontation, Evelyn grabbed Celeste as Celeste tried to leave. Celeste stepped backward through the now-unprotected opening and fell.
8. It was not a planned push, but Evelyn created the dangerous condition and lied about the confrontation.
9. Sebastian discovered what happened immediately and helped turn the death into an “accident” to protect Evelyn, their infant son Julian, and the Raven name.
10. In 1932 a transfer deed was forged moving Raven House from the dead Celeste to Sebastian.
11. Eleanor was taken away by Miriam and later married into the Mercer family. **Claire Mercer is Eleanor’s granddaughter** and Celeste’s direct descendant.
12. Evelyn confessed fragments to granddaughter **Talia** in 1978. Talia kept the confession secret.
13. In the late 1990s Eleanor contacted **Marcus Raven**, Adrian’s father. Marcus came to believe the Mercer line had a legitimate claim and left instructions that if Raven House was sold, a “Mercer” must be found.
14. In 2026 Adrian hires Claire because she is exceptionally qualified but also because his father’s sealed note names “Mercer.” Adrian does not initially know why.
15. Adrian withholds that motive from Claire; this becomes the core romantic betrayal.
16. Adrian’s cousin **Felix**, financially desperate for proceeds of the proposed sale, stages some current “hauntings,” steals an archive box, and tries to scare Claire away. He has nothing to do with the historical death.
17. Final evidence shows Evelyn caused the fatal circumstances; Sebastian covered it up; the title chain is defective.
18. Adrian chooses disclosure over concealment. Claire does not simply seize the house; the future is restructured around preservation, truthful provenance, and independently advised legal settlement.

## Central dramatic question
What do the living owe the truth when telling it may destroy the identities, property and relationships built on a lie?

## Emotional victory
**Truth without annihilation: the family survives, but the myth does not.**
