# Celeste Vane Timeline

| Year / Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1898 | Celeste Vane born. |
| 1917–1921 | Architecture training and work under men who receive much of the credit. |
| 1922 | Meets Sebastian Raven when he approaches her firm about a country house. |
| 1923 | Buys Blackmere site using Vane family money; deed is in her name. |
| 1924 | Marries Sebastian quietly at registry office. |
| 1925 | Raven House completed with experimental acoustic channels, service voids and concealed maintenance routes. |
| 1926 | Eleanor born; Miriam helps raise her away from the estate. |
| 1927 | Sebastian’s father threatens disinheritance. |
| 1928 | Sebastian publicly marries Evelyn without valid divorce. |
| 1929 | Celeste discovers Sebastian has represented Raven House as family property in financial dealings. |
| 1930 | Celeste assembles proof of marriage, ownership, Eleanor’s identity and Sebastian’s misuse. |
| Spring 1931 | Asks solicitor to prepare action asserting ownership and acknowledging Eleanor. |
| 17 Oct 1931, morning | Evelyn orders west stair safety gate removed. |
| 17 Oct 1931, evening | Evelyn confronts Celeste, grabs her wrist; Celeste steps backward through unguarded opening and falls. |
| 17 Oct 1931, later | Sebastian understands what happened and reports death as an accident. |
| 18–21 Oct 1931 | Papers disappear; servants are silenced. |
| 1932 | Forged deed transfers house to Sebastian using Celeste’s post-death forged signature. |
| 1932–1933 | Miriam leaves the region with Eleanor and selected papers. |
| 1956 | Sebastian writes unsent confession to Eleanor: admits cover-up and says Evelyn did not deliberately push Celeste. |
| 1978 | Elderly Evelyn partially confesses on cassette to Talia. |
| 1997–1999 | Eleanor contacts Marcus Raven and sends partial proof. |
| 2001 | Eleanor dies. |
| 2019 | Marcus dies, leaving sealed instruction: if Raven House is sold, “find Mercer.” |
| 2026 | Adrian prepares sale and hires Claire Mercer. |
