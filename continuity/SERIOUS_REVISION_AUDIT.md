# Serious Revision Audit

Branch: `serious-revision`

## Final status

**Serious manuscript revision is complete.**

The pass moved beyond continuity repair into a full developmental revision covering reveal order, character knowledge, architectural and legal plausibility, romance causality, safety logic, present-day sabotage, prose leakage and the reader/export pipeline.

This does **not** mean no future editor or beta reader may suggest changes. It means the novel no longer has known structural work outstanding from this pass. The next stage is external reader response, copy edit and publication proofing.

## Major structural repairs completed

1. **Mercer ancestry reveal restored to Chapter 23.** Earlier chapters establish that Eleanor becomes a Mercer and that the surname matters without proving Claire’s descent too soon.
2. **Adrian’s betrayal sharpened into informed-consent failure.** He did not secretly know Claire was the descendant; he knew enough Mercer context to understand that the uncertainty should have been disclosed before hiring and before intimacy.
3. **Felix cleanly separated from the historical crime.** His debt, copied key, staged disturbances, archive theft and passage entrapment are fully present-day acts.
4. **Historical death mechanism corroborated across independent evidence.** Gate photograph, carpenter’s daybook, surviving fixing marks, coroner’s wrist note, Evelyn’s tape, Celeste’s letter and Sebastian’s confession converge without requiring a planned push.
5. **Sebastian’s diary red herring made fair.** The line about ending what should never have begun resolves as the abandoned reconciliation/delay attempt rather than murder confession.
6. **Most apparent hauntings rationalised before the ending.** Speaking tubes, service voids, ventilation, later heating alterations, Elspeth and Felix explain the earlier events. The Chapter 5 “Eleanor” voice is traced in Chapter 31 to Talia speaking elsewhere in the house. Only the final “Claire” remains unexplained.
7. **Talia deepened from obstructionist to morally coherent keeper of silence.** Her logic is understandable, damaging and explicitly not excused.
8. **Evelyn made culpable without becoming a cartoon villain.** Her fear and precarious marital position are legible while the gate removal, restraint and silence remain hers.

## Plausibility repairs completed

### Property / legal
- 1923 Blackmere is treated as unregistered land proved by conveyance/deed history rather than a modern Land Registry entry.
- 1991 first registration and modern registered title are separated from the historic 1932 fraud question.
- The novel no longer implies that proving historic fraud automatically hands the house to Claire.
- Harcourt discovers the fatal execution-date problem only when the dormant predecessor deed packet is recovered during the modern investigation.
- The ending uses specialist counsel, separate representation, standstill/preservation structures and reserved legal rights rather than a romantic transfer of keys.

### Family / civil records
- Civil marriage index, historic certified copy and modern GRO-copy process are kept distinct.
- Eleanor’s 1956 marriage entry does not invent a crossed-out/deceased father notation.
- Miriam’s real-world care of Eleanor is not equated automatically with sole legal guardianship.
- Claire’s documentary ancestry is proved only after independent generational records converge in Chapter 23.

### Building / safety
- Service-void access is pre-surveyed and documented; Claire uses check-in and two-exit precautions before Felix deliberately defeats them.
- Potential asbestos/unknown historic board triggers stop-work and specialist survey before the Witness Room is opened.
- Acoustic effects arise from several plausible overlapping systems rather than an impossible whole-house mechanism.
- The west-stair gate is corroborated by photograph, fixing evidence and daybook entry.

### Police / financial
- Felix’s interview shifts from voluntary questioning to a caution and offer of legal advice once grounds to suspect him are clear.
- His motive relies on public company records and estate correspondence/payment directions he supplied himself, not implausible access to private banking data.

## Romance / emotional causality completed

- Friction becomes respect through work.
- Attraction grows through competence, humour and ordinary intimacy as well as danger.
- Claire chooses not to kiss Adrian after Chapter 13’s emotional discovery; he accepts the boundary.
- Chapter 15’s first kiss is deliberate and explicitly mutual.
- Chapter 20’s physical intimacy is Claire’s choice and is explicitly not forgiveness for Marcus-note withholding.
- Chapter 24’s breakup lands only after Claire can understand exactly what Adrian knew before the hire.
- Adrian does not win her back by rescue, suffering or apology.
- Chapters 25–33 maintain separation while his behaviour changes.
- He chooses disclosure, sale withdrawal and independent process before knowing whether Claire will forgive him.
- Reconciliation occurs only after legal/professional independence is already structural, not promised.
- Chapter 35 preserves Claire’s separate home, contract, solicitor and decision-making.

## Prose / reveal-control repairs completed

- Production clue codes and authorial chapter references identified in Draft prose were removed.
- Repeated generic-gothic phrasing was reduced in favour of building-specific sensory detail.
- Evidence language was narrowed where first-draft conclusions outran the record.
- Several explanatory passages were converted into action, procedure or interpersonal conflict.
- Major discoveries now have breathing room and independent corroboration rather than repeating the same reveal in adjacent chapters.
- The final supernatural note is one controlled exception rather than a retreat from the book’s rational architecture.
