# Evelyn Ashcombe Raven

- **Age:** 29 in 1931
- **Role:** Sebastian’s public wife

## Presentation
Socially perfect, strategic, deeply afraid of humiliation, abandonment and the loss of her son’s status.

## Secret / hidden information
Orders the west stair gate removed and physically confronts Celeste.

## Arc
Culpable but understandable; her fear explains rather than excuses her actions.
