# Talia Raven

- **Age:** 68
- **Role:** Adrian’s aunt / keeper of family memory

## Presentation
Elegant, intimidating, exacting. Believes silence can prevent harm.

## Secret / hidden information
Evelyn partially confessed to her in 1978. Talia later corresponded with Claire’s mother.

## Arc
Moves from gatekeeper to witness, accepting that secrecy has become another form of harm.

## Voice guardrails
- Dialogue should reflect education, temperament and social position without caricature.
- Avoid expositional speeches unless the character has a concrete reason to explain.
- Contradictions between what this character knows and what a chapter shows should be treated as continuity errors.
