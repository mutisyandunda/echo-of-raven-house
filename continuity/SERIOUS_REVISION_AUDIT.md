# Serious Revision Audit

Branch: `serious-revision`

## Pass 1 — structural continuity and reveal control

### Critical issues found and repaired
1. **Mercer ancestry was revealed too early.** The first draft confirmed or effectively confirmed Claire’s descent from Eleanor in Chapters 3, 16, 18, 19 and 20, collapsing the intended Chapter 23 reveal. **Repaired:** Chapters 1–22 may now establish that Eleanor became a Mercer and that the surname matters, but documentary proof that Eleanor is Claire’s grandmother is withheld until Chapter 23.
2. **Chapter 16 overpaid C20.** **Repaired:** `M. Mercer` is now an unidentified late-1990s correspondent; Talia’s knowledge is clear, the recipient’s identity is not.
3. **Chapter 18 overpaid the ancestry thread.** **Repaired:** it authenticates the marriage, identifies Eleanor Vane, and establishes that Eleanor later became a Mercer. The direct Claire link remains unresolved.
4. **Chapters 19–20 repeated the ancestry proof.** **Repaired:** Marcus’s note identifies `E.M.` as Eleanor Mercer and establishes title relevance; Chapter 20 proves Eleanor’s parentage and Miriam’s role but does not connect Eleanor to Claire.
5. **Timeline error in Chapter 16.** A letter described as dated 1979 was later described as late-1990s correspondence. **Repaired:** the filing mark is now 1999.
6. **Authorial production codes leaked into prose.** **Repaired in the identified chapters:** clue codes and explicit chapter-number references were removed from Chapters 4, 6, 9, 28, 29, 31 and 32.
7. **C9 object continuity mismatch.** Tracking documents said the certified marriage copy was in a drawing tube while prose placed it in a concealed tubular compartment inside Celeste’s desk. **Repaired in trackers:** the drawing tube contains the brass key; the marriage copy is in the desk tube.
8. **C21 tracker mismatch.** **Repaired:** Felix’s financial urgency is seeded at the family dinner in Chapter 6, proved by debt records in Chapter 26 and paid off in Chapter 27.
