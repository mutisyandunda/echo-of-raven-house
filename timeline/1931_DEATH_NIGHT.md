# 17 October 1931 — Death Night Reconstruction

## Morning
- Evelyn inspects west stair before evening gathering.
- She orders the removable brass safety gate taken out because it “spoils the line.”
- Carpenter records: “West gate removed, instruction Mrs E.A.”

## Evening confrontation
- Evelyn confronts Celeste near the west stair landing.
- Celeste intends to leave.
- Evelyn grabs Celeste’s wrist to stop her.
- Celeste steps backward.
- Because the safety gate has been removed, she passes through the open edge and falls.

## Immediate aftermath
- Evelyn understands that her earlier instruction created the fatal condition.
- Sebastian quickly understands enough of the truth.
- He chooses concealment over disclosure.
- The death is reported as an accident.

## Later distortion
- Coroner records wrist bruising but does not interpret it correctly.
- Papers vanish.
- Family narrative shifts toward Celeste having left years earlier.
- In 1932, forged title paperwork cleans the ownership chain on paper.
