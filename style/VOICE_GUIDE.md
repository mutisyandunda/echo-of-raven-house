# Voice Guide

## Narrative texture
Elegant, controlled, sensory but not purple. Precision should create atmosphere.

## Preferred techniques
- Material detail before emotional labeling.
- Let architecture reveal character.
- Use silence, interruption and spatial distance in dialogue scenes.
- Keep historical documents distinct in rhythm and diction.
- Romantic tension should often sit inside professional conversation.

## Claire POV
Notices measurements, joinery, paper quality, alterations, inconsistent geometry, wording in records, and what people omit.

## Adrian on the page
Understatement, dry humour, precise courtesy. Emotional intensity appears as restraint breaking rather than speeches.

## Gothic register
Use:
- echo
- void
- resonance
- draft
- lime plaster
- oxidised brass
- rain-dark glass
- warped timber
- paper dust
- acoustic dead spots

Avoid overusing:
- darkness
- shadow
- chill
- haunted
- eerie
- sinister
- “something felt wrong”
