## Pass 1 — structural continuity and reveal control

### Critical issues found
1. **Mercer ancestry is revealed too early.** The first draft confirms or effectively confirms Claire’s descent from Eleanor in Chapters 3, 16, 18, 19 and 20, which collapses the intended Chapter 23 reveal. Revision target: Chapters 1–22 may establish that Eleanor became a Mercer and that the surname matters, but **must not prove Eleanor is Claire’s grandmother until Chapter 23**.
2. **Chapter 16 overpays C20.** The chapter should seed an unidentified `M. Mercer` correspondence and Talia’s knowledge, not identify Margaret or establish Claire’s maternal line.
3. **Chapter 18 overpays the ancestry thread.** It should authenticate the marriage, identify Eleanor Vane, and establish that Eleanor later became a Mercer; the direct Claire link remains unresolved.
4. **Chapters 19–20 repeat the ancestry proof.** Marcus’s note may identify E.M. as Eleanor Mercer and establish that Mercer matters to title, but the personal lineage proof must wait for Miriam’s packet in Chapter 23.
5. **Timeline error in Chapter 16.** A letter described as dated 1979 is later described as late-1990s correspondence. The seed will be moved to the late 1990s.
6. **Authorial production codes leak into prose.** Remove `C4`, `C13`, `C21`, `R1`, `R7` and explicit references such as “Chapter One/Two/Eight” from the reader-facing manuscript.
7. **C9 object continuity mismatch.** Tracking documents say the certified marriage copy is in a drawing tube; prose places it in a concealed tubular compartment inside Celeste’s desk. Canon/tracking should match the prose mechanism.
8. **C21 tracker mismatch.** Felix’s financial urgency is seeded at the family dinner in Chapter 6, not first in Chapter 26.
