# The Echo of Raven House

Canonical production repository for the gothic romantic mystery **The Echo of Raven House**.

## Story promise
A modern architectural archivist is hired to catalogue a remote English estate before sale and discovers that the building itself has been engineered to conceal evidence of a secret marriage, a stolen inheritance, and a death the Raven family spent nearly a century misdescribing.

**Core conceit:** architecture functions as forensic evidence.

## Working rules
1. `/canon` is authoritative.
2. Chapter prose must not contradict canon without first changing canon.
3. Mystery evidence is tracked from seed → interpretation → payoff.
4. Reader knowledge and character knowledge are tracked separately.
5. Romance progression is structural, not improvised.
6. Raven House should feel uncanny, but almost every phenomenon receives a plausible architectural explanation.
7. The final unexplained echo remains genuinely unexplained.

## Target
- Genre: Gothic romantic mystery / family-secret suspense
- Setting: Northumberland, England, 2026
- Historical span: 1922–1984, with later family correspondence through 2019
- Primary POV: close third, mainly Claire Mercer
- Length: ~90,000 words
- Chapters: 35
