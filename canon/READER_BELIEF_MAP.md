# Reader Belief Map

## Chapters 1–5
Likely belief: Raven House may be haunted; its architecture is concealing something.

## Chapters 6–10
Likely belief: there was a hidden child or affair; Talia and Harcourt are covering a family scandal.

## Chapters 11–17
Likely belief: Sebastian betrayed Celeste and the house conceals proof.

## Chapters 18–22
Likely belief: Celeste was the legal wife and the Raven family may have stolen the estate.

## Chapters 23–27
Likely belief: Claire is the heir, Adrian may have manipulated her, and someone in the present is actively obstructing the investigation.

## Chapters 28–30
Likely belief: Evelyn probably murdered Celeste and Talia protected the crime.

## Chapters 31–32
Corrected belief: Sebastian did not push Celeste, but he covered Evelyn’s culpable confrontation and the forged aftermath.

## Chapters 33–35
Final moral frame: the central issue is no longer who “wins” Raven House, but what the living owe truth.
